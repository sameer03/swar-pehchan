Find it

Always tuned to your Sa. For the sound of a real instrument, load a recording of a tanpura (any key). The app finds its Sa, retunes it and loops it, and remembers it in this browser. When you start listening with the tanpura on, stay quiet for 3 seconds while the app learns to ignore it. Headphones give the cleanest result.

Reading the notation

  • Re underline = komal (flat): Re, Ga, Dha, Ni
  • Ma tick above = tivra (sharp) Ma
  • Ni dot below = mandra (lower octave)
  • Sa dot above = taar (upper octave)
  • GaRe small raised = kan swar (grace note)
  • Pa⁀Ga arc = meend (slide)

Copied text uses Re(k) for komal, Ma(t) for tivra, .Ni for mandra and Sa' for taar, [Ga]Re for a kan swar and Pa~Ga for meend.
